Loci · 地灵

A pocket spirit of place that sees where you are and tells you the city's stories.

一只会看、会说的口袋地灵，陪你在城市里解谜，讲这个地方的故事。

Source on GitHub

Genius loci — the spirit of a place. 人杰地灵. Every city has one.

How it plays

  1. A clue sends you into the streets: find the date carved on a gate, the name on a stone.
  2. Point your spirit at what you found. It looks.
  3. If you're really there and you got it right, it tells you that place's story — and the next clue.

答案只在现场。AI 搜不到，地灵得看见你真的在那里。

What it's made of

A body that sees and speaksSeeed SenseCAP Watcher — a round screen with eyes, a camera, a microphone, a speaker. Or a thumb-sized XIAO ESP32S3 Sense for wearables.
A brain that judgesA small open-source relay asks a vision model one question — did they find it? — and turns the story into speech.
A route you can writeEach route is a plain file: stations, riddles, targets, stories. The stories are written by people, not generated.

Status
